Tutor, and lecturer for the even-numbered weeks: file identity, Save As,
the diff, authority, conversion damage, and retention.

Builds and maintains the packages the labs and assessments inspect. The
conflicting timestamps in them are deliberate and reproducible; if you
find an inconsistency that looks like a mistake rather than a plant,
report it, because both outcomes are useful and one of them is a bug.

Background in digital preservation, which is why the course treats a
format migration as a decision about which losses to accept rather than
as maintenance.
