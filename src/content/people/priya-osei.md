Runs the file clinic: a walk-in slot where a student brings a real
duplicate problem from their own storage and leaves with it classified,
on the spot, using the same four tests the course teaches. A number of
Week 4's Duplicate Audits started as a clinic case.

Keeps no record of what students bring. The clinic classifies; it does
not grade, and it does not remember your files once the session ends.
