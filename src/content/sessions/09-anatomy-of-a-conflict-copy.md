## Before the session

Search your own storage for conflict copies. The naming varies by service:
`conflicted copy`, `(1)`, `Conflict`, a device name in parentheses, a date
appended. Search for a few patterns rather than one. Record the full
filename verbatim, including punctuation and spacing. The filename is the
service's incident report, and it is the only report it filed.

## In the session

Read each filename for what it discloses: which device or account lost, and
what the service used as the timestamp. Then open both versions and
determine whether they actually differ. Some conflict copies are byte-
identical to the file they were forked from: the service saw a conflict in
its bookkeeping that didn't exist in the content. Note those separately;
they are a different finding from a real divergence.

## Afterwards

For any pair that genuinely differs, state the difference in one sentence,
and say which version you would have kept. Then note how long the conflict
copy had been sitting there before this exercise made you open it. That
interval is the actual measure of what the service's report was worth.
