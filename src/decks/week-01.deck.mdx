# final_FINAL_revised2.docx

Not written by someone who doesn't understand version control.

---

## One string, several claims

- supersedes `final.docx`
- a second round happened after that
- as of now, this is the one to use

All of that, in a filename. Nowhere else to put it.

---

## A workaround, not a failure

No shared field for "which one is current."

The name is the only field everyone can see and edit.

`final` is a claim. Not a guarantee.

---

## Two different things

**Versioning** — tracking what's current. As old as revision itself.

**Version control** — software that does it automatically. About sixty
years old.

Most people writing documents have never opened the second one.

---
