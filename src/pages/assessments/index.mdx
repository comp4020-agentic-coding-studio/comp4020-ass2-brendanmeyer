Four pieces of graded work, no essays. Each one produces evidence rather than
an argument about evidence: a genealogy of files you already have, a
reconstructed timeline, a merge with its reasoning attached, a chain of
converted artefacts. Two of the four use your own files; the others use
supplied packages.

In every one of them, the finding you cannot establish is worth marks. Say
what you could not determine and what would have settled it. An answer that
resolves everything is usually an answer that stopped checking.
